# About Myself 

My name is Rajesh Krishnan.

My nationality is American, and my ethnicity Asian Indian.
My primary languages are English, Tamil, and Hindi; my knowledge of some other Indic languages is at a rudimentary level. 

Prior to retirement, my professional work involved computers and communication networks. A list of my publications and patents can be found on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=aMfW5dMAAAAJ&pagesize=100&sortby=pubdate).

My practice includes veganism, minimalism, [yoga](https://rajesh-krishnan.github.io/pys), and advaita vedanta.
