# About Myself 

My name is Rajesh Krishnan. My nationality is American (USA), and my origin is in India.

My languages are English, Tamil, and Hindi primarily, and include a few other Indic languages at a rudimentary level. 

Prior to retirement, my professional work involved computers and communication networks. A list of my patents, publications, and technical reports can be found on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=aMfW5dMAAAAJ&pagesize=100&sortby=pubdate).

My practice includes veganism, minimalism, yoga (as taught by Maharishi [Patanjali](https://rajesh-krishnan.github.io/pys)), and vedanta (as taught by Jagadguru Shankaracharya).
