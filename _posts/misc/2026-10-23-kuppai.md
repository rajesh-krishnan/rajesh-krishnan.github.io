## எனதரிதலால் யானறிதல் - கோவிறை

குப்பையைப்பெருக்கிக்கூட்டும் மக்கு

மறுவேறுமீள்பயனிக்  குறைமறுமக்கு 


## Translation

**Through cutting (what's) mine, knowing (who am) I** - *Kovirai (Pen name translated from Rajesh)*

(You), the fool who sweeps and collects (alternative gloss: multiplies and adds/accumulates) garbage

reuse, repurpose, recycle, reduce, refuse, compost


## Commentary

This couplet on garbage was inspired by my cousin Shri. Hariharan/Kannan who said: "all this is kuppai (garbage)."  The context was a philsophical and scholarly discussion which ultimately will not lead to ultimate wisdom.
 
The title and the pun suggest that the couplet may pack multiple intents. So, what is *kuppai* here? 

   - **Kuppai is garbage, items no longer considered as mine, which must be swept, collected, and discarded** 
      - Composting and the five Rs (C+5R) are familiar injunctions 
      - *A message of eco-friendly minimalism* 

  - **Kuppai is all the things and wealth considered as mine, which have multiplied and accumulated, and which must be swept, collected, and discarded**   
       - Reduce consumption and waste of material, money, and time; also donate money, give away things, and volunteer your time
       - Likewise for the other C+5R injunctions
       - *A message of yogic non-possessiveness (aparigraha yama) intertwined with donation (dana) and service (seva)* 

    - **Kuppai is the impurity in thought, speech, and action, which have multiplied and accumulated, and which must be swept, collected, and discarded**
        - Refuse physical and virtual consumption that multiplies and adds; also refuse harmful ideas, words, and habits
        - Likewise for the other C+5R injunctions
        - *A message of yogic cleanliness (saucha niyama), both external and internal*
  
    - **Kuppai is the sumtotal of things, emotions, concepts, identities, memories, and relationships that we mistake as I or mine**
       - Composting and the five Rs are merely witnessed once this is understood 
       - *A message of renunciation (tyaga) and wisdom (jnana)*

So what *kuppai* are you dealing with today, other than this blog, that is?
