## Objective

The objective of this side project is a bijective map from Sanskrit to Tamil of the word in Patanjali's yoga sutra. 
A naive application of the map to the full text will confirm coverage.
Preferences include minimizing loan words, preserving word order and matching case (vibhakti/vettrumai) when applying the map.

Non-goals include:  

    - completion by a fixed date
    - commentary or interpretation
    - perfect lexical matching for semantics and connotation
    - preserving number, gender, tense-moods, or syllabic length 
    - alignment with modern or classical usage, style, or prosody
    - usable, idiomatic, pedagogical, or comprehensible translation
    - alignment to established translations of terms or literary precedents

My gratitude to Patanjali and all the scholars of Tamil, Sanskrit, and English, and may they forgive any errors in spelling, grammar, and meaning.
My prayers to Ishvara to bless this work.
